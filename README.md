Buying used cars is a tricky process. Scams, forged maintenance records, and misleading information are far too common in the world of secondhand vehicles. **Honesty** aims to change that.

### 🚗 What is Honesty?

**Honesty** is a simple, no BS tool that provides clear and honest information about secondhand cars to help car buyers verify key information about a vehicle before making a purchase. Have a modified car, or did a lot of work yourself? Honesty can help document your work that isnt documented officially on tools like Carfax.

### ✨ Features

- Detailed service history with part invoices, job durations, and price breakdown
- Modification history to know what you're buying into
- 3D LIDAR scan viewer to look at the car from any angle, even far away
